TSI Study Hall

Practice for the TSIA2 math test, one strand at a time.

What the test covers

The TSIA2 math test has four strands: Quantitative Reasoning, Algebraic Reasoning, Geometric & Spatial Reasoning, and Probabilistic & Statistical Reasoning. The practice blocks below cover all four.

How to use these pages:
  • Each day has bellwork, a short lesson, in-class practice, and homework with an answer bank. Every correct answer appears in the bank exactly once, so you can check yourself.
  • Do the bellwork without a calculator when the page says so.
  • Official sample questions from the test maker are free at College Board’s TSIA2 resources page.

Individual plans, Fall 2026

Every student in the study hall gets a plan built from their own diagnostic, not a class-wide packet. Student names are replaced with letters.

The diagnostics

How the two diagnostics were built

I wrote both 16-question diagnostics and built them in Eduphoria Aware in October 2026. They are currently in use: I give them in my own TSI study hall, and the district is using them across its campuses, not only at mine. Every item carries two alignments, a Texas College and Career Readiness Standard (CCRS) and the matching TEKS, plus a depth-of-knowledge level, so a missed question points to a specific skill. Diagnostic 1 covers 10 CCRS standards and 13 TEKS; Diagnostic 2 covers 11 and 16.

Getting standards onto items took most of the summer. In June and July 2026 I worked with Eduphoria support to import my existing question banks with standards attached. Along the way I checked the CCRS mathematics set in Aware against the state’s published documents and found it was the original 2009 edition: 70 standards in ten sections, where the 2018 revision has 65 in nine. I reported it on July 14. Eduphoria confirmed that the set in their system was the 2009 edition and sent it to their product team, and by the end of July told me the 2018 Math and Reading CCRS had been added, with the work still in progress. I also ran a six-version test import that isolated why standards were being dropped without any error, which led Eduphoria to send the manifest format their importer accepts.

The diagnostics are tagged with the CCRS codes available in Aware when I built them, which follow the 2009 numbering.

  • Diagnostic 1 and Diagnostic 2: both tests are in active use in the district this year, so the questions are not posted here. Copies are available on request to schools and evaluators.
How a plan is built

From a 16-question diagnostic to a two-page plan

The diagnostic has four questions per strand. Each student’s missed skills are put in priority order: basics first, then by how heavily the TSIA2 weights the strand. A student with five or fewer right gets three targets; everyone else gets four.

  • Each target gives the move, one worked example, and the mistake to watch for, tagged to its TEKS and lesson code.
  • Page 2 is a practice row for each target, with a self-check answer strip.
  • A four-day plan ends with a re-check: three in a row on a target signs it off.
  • Diagnostic 1 item analysis and intervention plans (11 pages): class-wide gaps, the student-by-item table, ten class days of openers and table work, and the skill cards. It describes the test questions in detail, so it is available on request while the diagnostic is in use.
  • All 17 individual plans in one file
  • One plan each, Student: A · B · C · D · E · F · G · H · I · J · K · L · M · N · O · P · Q
